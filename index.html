Privacy Policy

Daily Installment Reminder

Effective Date: August 2026

1. Data Collection
We collect only the data you provide: customer names, phone numbers, installment details, your staff profile information, and optional public chat posts (hiring/job search requests). No data is collected automatically.
2. Data Storage
Customer/installment data is stored locally on your device using browser localStorage. Public chat (Hiring & Job Search) posts are stored in the free Firebase Firestore tier (shared board visible to all users of the app) and auto-deleted after exactly 15 days (100% backend deletion via Firestore .delete() + client filter).
3. Public Chat Board
The Chat section is a public shared board: all staff/hiring personnel can see every hiring offer and job search request. Anyone can view, filter (by location/position/company), and contact via call or WhatsApp. Posts auto-expire after 15 days and are permanently removed from backend.
4. SMS Sending
SMS messages are sent directly from your phone's SIM using the Web SMS API when available. We do not intercept or store the content of messages you send.
5. Staff Data Transfer
When you export or import profiles, the data remains under your full control. Imported profiles are kept completely separate and never mixed with existing data.
6. No Third-Party Sharing
We do not sell or share your personal data. Public chat content is intentionally visible within the app community only.
7. Your Control
You can delete individual customers, centers, profiles, or your own chat posts at any time. Expired posts are auto-cleaned on backend.
8. Contact
For any privacy-related questions, please contact the app owner.
